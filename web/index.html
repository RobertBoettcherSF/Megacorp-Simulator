Paths, ped motion, action queues, and shots are precomputed by Ada. Play, pause, and step replay that trace. The marker is the ped and the stroke is its facing. A bar above a ped is health. A red shot line is a hit and a dashed line is a miss. The gold diamond is an item until it is picked up. A click snaps to the nearest prepared start or goal.
