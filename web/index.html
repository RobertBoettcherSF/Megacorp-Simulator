Paths, ped motion, action queues, shots, and vehicles are precomputed by Ada. Play, pause, and step replay that trace. The marker is the ped and the stroke is its facing. A bar above a ped is health. A red shot line is a hit and a dashed line is a miss. The gold diamond is an item until it is picked up. A teal rectangle is a car and an amber one is a van. Grey means a wreck. On the behaviour trace, colour is the role: gold civilian, orange guard, blue police, red hostile. The word in brackets is that ped's mood. The mission trace lists each objective as open or done. A click snaps to the nearest prepared start or goal. The layout is a generated city. The world map is drawn from the Ada module in the browser when it loads, and the day counter moves. Reload continues that day. New world starts again. Import stores a tileset or sprite only after the Ada module accepts it. The city beside the world map is ticked by Ada. Pause, 1x, 2x, and 4x also advance the campaign. Hover or tap a region for its owner, income, and the corporation list. Click a region to take its mission. Tap a person, then tap the marked tile, to finish it. Tap an enemy to attack. Win or lose returns that result to the world map. A click on a region opens that city's precomputed trace.
